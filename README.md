🌟 Tribute Page – Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Level 2 – Task 2
📌 About the Project
This project is a Tribute Page dedicated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, the 11th President of India and one of the most inspiring leaders in Indian history.
The webpage is designed using HTML and CSS to showcase his life, achievements, and contributions in a clean and visually appealing layout.
👨‍🚀 About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam
Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was an aerospace scientist and the 11th President of India. He played a major role in India's missile development programs and earned the title "Missile Man of India."
He was widely admired for his humility, dedication to education, and vision for transforming India into a developed nation.
